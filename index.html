Before you come

What should we bring?

Nothing. Towels, bathrobes and slippers are included for every guest.

Is it really only us?

Yes. During your slot the sauna, jacuzzi, relax room and shower are yours alone. Nobody else is booked in at the same time.

Can we come at night?

Yes. Lumen is open 24/7 and night slots are booked like any other. The free times are in the calendar.

How do we get in?

You let yourselves in with a personal door code for your booking. How and when it reaches you is described in your booking confirmation.

Is there an age limit?

Yes, Lumen is for guests aged 18 and over.

Can more than two of us come?

Yes, for a surcharge per extra person. The amount and the maximum number of guests are confirmed when you book.

What if we need to cancel?

The cancellation terms are confirmed when you book.

Is the sauna right for everyone?

If you are pregnant or have a heart or blood-pressure condition, please check with your doctor before using the sauna or hot tub.
